# Nexo v2.2.5

macOS self-update fix, plus a cleaner app and device.

## What's fixed
- **macOS self-update now works even from a translocated / read-only copy.**
  If Nexo is running from Gatekeeper's temporary path (for example launched
  straight out of the download), tapping *Update* now installs the new build
  into `/Applications` and relaunches from there instead of refusing. There is
  also a **Move to /Applications & restart** button on the About tab, and the
  updater writes a log to `~/Library/Logs/Nexo/updater.log`.
- **Back button added** to the pushed screens (Firmware update, New/Edit note,
  Add/Edit homework) so you can always navigate back.

## What's changed
- **MP3 player removed** from the device menu and the app's Music tab (and its
  Wi-Fi upload screen). Voice memos remain the way to capture and keep audio.

## Tested on real hardware
- Firmware builds and flashes to an M5Stack Cardputer-Adv; native tests pass
  (24/24).
- App builds for macOS and Android; `flutter analyze` clean.
