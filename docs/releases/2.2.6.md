# Nexo v2.2.6

Voice memos in the app — listen, save and transcribe the recordings from your
Cardputer.

## What's new
- **Voice memos screen.** On the Device tab, open **Voice memos** to pull the
  recordings from the device's SD card over Wi-Fi: play them in-app, save or
  share them, or delete them from the device.
- **Transcription on macOS.** Apple's Speech framework transcribes a memo
  in-app (long memos are split into chunks). On other platforms the app hands
  the memo to the system share sheet so you can send it to a transcription app.

## What's changed
- Firmware adds `/api/memos` endpoints (list, download, delete) so the app can
  read the memos directly.

## Tested on real hardware
- Firmware builds and flashes to an M5Stack Cardputer-Adv; native tests pass
  (24/24).
- App builds for macOS and Android; `flutter analyze` clean.
