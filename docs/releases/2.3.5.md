# Nexo v2.3.5

Home "TODAY" fix and real screen-timeout power saving.

## What's fixed

- **TODAY never showed tasks due today.** The Home pane computed a 0-based local
  day and compared it with 1-based `dueEpochDay`, so homework due today only
  appeared the following day. The comparison now uses the same 1-based day.
- **Clock screensaver showed yesterday's date.** The flip clock passed its
  0-based day to `dueDayToIso()`, which expects a 1-based day. Corrected, so the
  clock and Home pane now agree.
- **Screen timeout setting was ignored.** Firmware always slept after a fixed
  45 s. It now uses the app's **Screen timeout** setting:
  - `Never` keeps the panel on.
  - `15 / 30 / 60 / 120 seconds` dim the screen at one third of the timeout and
    turn the panel off at the timeout.
  - The flip-clock screensaver appears halfway through the timeout and also
    turns the panel off at the end instead of staying lit indefinitely.
- **No rendering while asleep.** All drawing and animation paths are skipped
  while the panel is off; the first key only wakes the device.
