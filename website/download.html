Installation

Firmware

  • Launcher: copy Nexo-m5stack-cardputer.bin into the Launcher apps folder on the SD card.
  • Raw flash: write the image to offset 0x10000 with esptool or PlatformIO.

Music

  • Copy MP3/WAV files into the /music folder of the SD card (or upload them from the app over Wi-Fi).

Companion app

  • Pair over Bluetooth, enter the device PIN, and Nexo syncs your notes and homework automatically.
  • The app checks GitHub Releases on launch and can update itself.
