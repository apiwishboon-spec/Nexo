Nexo keeps your notes, homework and voice memos on the M5Stack Cardputer — offline-first, always yours.

What it does

Everything lives on the device

Notes

Write and edit notes with a real keyboard. They persist on the Cardputer and mirror to the app.

Homework

Track subjects, due dates and status, and sync changes between the device and your phone.

Memos

Record voice memos to the SD card, play them back on the device, and transcribe them in the app.

Bluetooth

A NimBLE GATT server with PIN auth. One companion at a time — close Nexo on other devices before connecting.

Wi-Fi

An optional access point serves a small built-in web UI plus an API for bulk transfers and firmware updates.

OTA

Update the firmware over the air, and keep the app current with one-tap self-updates.

How it works

Device first, app second

1 · Notes & homework on the go

Type directly on the Cardputer. Everything is saved to on-device flash with atomic writes — no account, no server.

2 · Sync when you're together

Open the app and it reconciles over Bluetooth, merging offline edits with revisions and tombstones.

3 · Move files over Wi-Fi

Flip on the access point to browse your notes and homework from any browser, or push bulk files over the API.

4 · Update in place

Flash new firmware over the air, and let the app update itself from GitHub Releases.
